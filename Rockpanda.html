Rock Panda

Team 1 UI/UX Designer · 4 Software Engineers
Outcome First Place — Hsinchu × MeiChu Hackathon

Summary

Rock Panda started as a project during the Hsinchu × MeiChu Hackathon in partnership with Line Corporation. The project was student-centric, focusing on addressing common academic needs such as exam preparation and course material assistance.

Our team developed an intelligent Linebot using RAG (Retrieval Augmented Generation) technology. The bot serves as a digital teaching assistant — allowing students to access past exam questions, generate similar ones, and submit anonymous inquiries when they have questions about course content.

The project received First Place from Line Corporation and an overall First Place award in the competition.
